This is a game specifically for Hack Club Haven Mirpurkhas
ball like aliens have invaded your world and you are the one who has to defend it
the only weapon you have are canons and your only ammo are sweets
You have to shoot rasgullas (a traditional pakistani sweet) at the angry *bouncing* aliens who are trying to stomp you
Your only controls are A and D to move left and right and you have to aim your mouse to align your shot and press LMB to shoot
This game is made by 3 pakistani developers Rahoom, Jaswant and Noor M
Everything you sense is made by us, the background images, the sprites, the animations, the characters, the physics, the background music, the sound effects
